missions & heists

A new job posts itself the moment you're free.

Six mission types share one contract board. Whenever you're not already on a job, the game offers you a fresh one on a timer — a marker appears in the world and on the minimap, and a deadline starts ticking.

delivery

Delivery run

Reach a marker placed well across the city before the clock runs out. The baseline job — weighted to show up most often — with a reward that scales with the distance you had to cover.

circuit

Checkpoint circuit

Four to six checkpoints, spaced apart so the route actually crosses the city, each with its own deadline pressure baked into one overall timer. Clear them all before time expires.

steal

Steal contract

One specific parked car is marked — not any car, that car. Get to it and take it within 60 seconds before someone else does, or it's gone.

ram

Ram job

A moving target vehicle is marked for wrecking. Run it off the road within 45 seconds — a straightforward, aggressive contract with a shorter fuse.

escort

VIP escort

Reach a VIP on foot at a pickup marker, then get them safely to a destination marker across town. Drive carelessly near them and the contract fails outright — this is the one job where speed and caution actually compete with each other.

One shared slot

Only one mission is ever active at a time. Fail or finish it, and the board waits a short cooldown before offering the next.

Rewards scale with the risk.

  • Delivery and circuit payouts grow with the distance you actually had to travel — a marker across the map pays more than one around the corner.
  • Steal, ram and escort contracts pay a flat bounty, because the difficulty there is in finding and reaching one specific target, not the distance.
  • Every completed mission also counts toward your lifetime delivery total — five of them unlocks the Courier achievement.
  • Score earned here is the only thing that pays for a garage-saved car's fuel or a paint shop's silence — missions fund your evasion.
