controls & accessibility

Keyboard, touchscreen, or turn the whole thing down.

Every action maps to one key, coarse-pointer devices get on-screen touch pads automatically, and three pause-menu knobs let you tune the city's traffic, police aggression, or switch off the wanted system entirely.

01

keyboard

Every key, listed before you start.

  • W A S D / ← ↑ ↓ → walk on foot, drive in a car
  • E enter or exit the nearest car
  • SPACE handbrake — the key to every drift
  • F punch, or swing the bat if you're carrying one
  • H honk the horn
  • P pause — opens the settings panel below
  • M mute or unmute all sound
  • R restart — new city, score to zero, save untouched
  • 1 / 2 / 3 while paused: traffic density, police aggression, peaceful mode

Traffic density — key 1

Cycles moving and parked car counts, plus pedestrian population, between 0.5×, 1× and 1.5× the default. Takes effect on your next restart, so you can plan a quieter or busier city ahead of time.

Police aggression — key 2

Scales cop speed, spawn rate and helicopter closing speed between 0.5×, 1× and 1.5×. Takes effect immediately — you'll feel a more or less aggressive pursuit on your very next crime.

Peaceful mode — key 3

Turns the wanted system off entirely: heat drops to zero, every cop despawns, and no future crime raises it again until you toggle it back. A genuinely different, lower-stakes way to explore the same city.

On a phone or tablet? The pads show up on their own.

Any coarse-pointer device automatically gets a D-pad on the left for movement and action buttons on the right — accelerate, steer, brake, handbrake, enter/exit, and punch. No separate mobile build, no app store; the same file adapts.

Built to stand down gracefully.

  • Pausing the tab pauses the game — the simulation checks page visibility and stops advancing while you're elsewhere.
  • Every control is keyboard-first and listed on the start overlay before you ever press a key.
  • Sound is a single, sticky mute toggle — no separate volume sliders to hunt for.
  • Peaceful mode exists specifically for players who want the driving and the city without the pursuit.
