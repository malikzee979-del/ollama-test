five to chase

Achievements, unlocked once, kept forever.

  • First Steal — borrow your first car.
  • Drifter — lay down 5 kilometers of skid marks across every drift you've ever pulled.
  • Most Wanted — survive 20 total seconds at five stars.
  • Courier — complete 5 missions.
  • Collector — save your first car to the garage.

What's actually in your save.

  • Your all-time best score — shown on the HUD stat line every run, never reset by R.
  • Lifetime stats: total steals, deliveries, distance drifted, seconds spent at five stars, and cars saved to the garage.
  • Which of the five achievements you've unlocked, permanently.
  • Your garage-saved car's accel, top speed, grip, paint color and health — one JSON object, one car.

The honest footnote: it's localStorage, scoped to this browser on this machine. Clear site data, switch browsers, or open a private window, and it's gone — there's no account or cloud sync to fall back on.
