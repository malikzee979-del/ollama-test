One city. Seven ways to break the law in it.

The open world

96×96 tiles of hashed city, deterministic on every reload: a river with real bridges, a stadium block, parks, sidewalks, and four corner services tucked into the grid.

Explore the city →

Vehicles & driving

Drift-on-handbrake physics, per-car handling rolls, headlights and brake lights, damage and wrecks, gas stations that repair for cash.

See how driving feels →

Missions & heists

Delivery runs, checkpoint circuits, steal contracts, ram jobs and a VIP escort — a new job posts itself whenever you're between crimes.

Read the contract board →

The wanted system

Zero to five stars, roadblocks from three, a helicopter from four. Hide in a park, respray at the paint shop, or bribe the corner mart.

Learn to evade →

Combat

A punch on foot, a bat if you find one, pedestrians who scatter and cops who get briefly stunned — comedy, not gore.

See the moveset →

Progression

A high score, five achievements, lifetime stats, and a garage that saves your car's paint and handling to this browser between runs.

Controls & accessibility

Full keyboard scheme, on-screen touch pads on phones, three difficulty knobs, and a peaceful mode that turns the heat off entirely.
