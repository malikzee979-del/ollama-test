the wanted system

Local police, enforcing local law.

Every crime — running someone over, being spotted stealing a car, assaulting an officer — adds heat. Heat brings cops, cops escalate to roadblocks and a helicopter, and none of it goes anywhere near a real network. It's still entirely a local, in-browser simulation.

★★★★★

Five stars of heat

Wanted level runs 0–5. It rises one star per fresh crime (up to the cap) and decays one star at a time once you've stayed clean for a few seconds — faster in a park, slower under a circling helicopter.

Cops that predict, not chase

Police steer toward where you're about to be, not where you are — lead prediction that makes a straight-line escape much harder than it looks.

Roadblocks at three stars

Once wanted hits three, parked cruisers start appearing ahead of your direction of travel, forcing you off your line instead of just chasing from behind.

A helicopter at four

Four stars brings a helicopter that tracks you from above and actively suppresses your heat decay while it's nearby — the strongest reason to break line of sight.

What actually raises heat

A pedestrian hit, a witnessed car theft, or landing a hit on an officer — the last one alone can push you a full star in one swing.

Getting busted

Let a cop close within arm's reach and it's over for this pursuit: wanted resets to zero, you're returned to spawn on foot — but score, stats and achievements are untouched.

Difficulty is a dial, not fixed

Police aggression is a settings knob (0.5×/1×/1.5×) cycled from the pause menu — it scales cop speed, spawn rate, and how fast a helicopter closes in.

three ways to cool off

Getting away is a choice, not just outrunning them.

Go to ground in a park

Standing in a park with no cop nearby quadruples the rate your heat decays — the fastest, cheapest way to cool down, provided you can shake a tail long enough to reach one.

Pay the paint shop

Two seconds parked at a paint shop drops two stars instantly and gives your car a new color — a fresh coat and new plates the local police apparently can't see through.

Bribe the corner mart

On foot, with enough score, the corner mart will quietly remove one star for a flat fee — and lets your cooldown timer resume immediately instead of waiting out the usual grace period.

Or just turn the heat off.

Peaceful mode is one keypress away in the pause menu: wanted drops to zero, every cop despawns, and no crime ever raises heat again until you flip it back — a whole different way to enjoy the same city.
