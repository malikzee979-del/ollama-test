## Processing

Clips were converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV, peak-normalized (0.89), quiet
material boosted with soft limiting, and loopable beds (engines, siren, rain)
made seamless with equal-power end-to-head crossfades. The processing scripts
live outside the project; re-deriving them is documented in git history.
